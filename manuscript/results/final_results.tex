\section{Results}
\label{sec:results}

\subsection{Census outcomes}
The census comprises $24\times3=72$ chunk--arm attempts, of which
50 were admitted and 22 failed verification.

\subsection{Mechanical admission}
\label{sec:admission}
Table~\ref{tab:admission} reports $A(c,a)$, the share of chunks whose
returned item reproduces against its own bundle. The quantity is
computed entirely by the verifier, so it is reproducible from the
released records.

\begin{table}[!t]
\caption{Mechanical admission $A$ over the 24-chunk frame. An attempt
is admitted only if its returned evidence tuple recomputes against the
chunk's own bundle.}
\label{tab:admission}
\centering
\begin{tabular}{lccc}
\toprule
Arm & Admitted & Rate & Contract violations \\
\midrule
ECM (contracted)      & 22/24 & 0.92 & 2 \\
Direct                & 16/24 & 0.67 & 7 \\
Structured, no contract & 12/24 & 0.50 & 9 \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{table}

Both paired contrasts are significant. Against the direct arm, 6
chunks are admitted only under the contract and none in the opposite
direction, giving an exact McNemar $p=0.031$. Against the structured
no-contract arm there are 11 against 1, giving $p=0.006$. Under Holm
control at family $\alpha=0.05$ the smaller $p$ is compared with
$0.025$ and the larger with $0.05$, so both contrasts reject. The
contract therefore raises the rate at which a single call yields a
source-consistent item, with each chunk as its own control.

\subsection{Failure structure}
\label{sec:failures}
Of the 22 failures, 18 violate the verbatim-quotation condition
$q\sqsubseteq T_c$, 2 return no single decodable JSON object, and 2
return a malformed field set. The verbatim violations fall into three
kinds.

In ten attempts the quotation is text rendered \emph{inside the page
image} and absent from the recognised text: independent recognition of
each figure crop matches eight of the quotations to the image text
completely and two in substantial part. This is the failure mode the
verbatim rule is designed to prevent; it occurs 4 times in the direct
arm and 6 times in the structured no-contract arm, and never in the
contracted arm.

Four further attempts differ from the source only in the
capitalisation of the first character; the gate normalises Unicode
composition and whitespace but not letter case, so they are rejected.
Admitting them post hoc would give $23/24$, $17/24$, and $14/24$ with
$p=0.031$ and $p=0.012$, leaving the direction, the ordering, and both
rejections unchanged.

The remaining four quotations are composed or paraphrased rather than
copied, spread across the three arms.
