\begin{abstract}
We present ECM--TQAG, a protocol for generating evidence-grounded
multimodal question--answer items from scanned documents. For each
source chunk the protocol fixes a conditioning bundle of recognised
text, document structure, and hash-bound figure crops; an evidence
contract then requires the generator, within a single call, to commit
to a verbatim text quotation, an answer planned before the question,
and a figure identified by content hash, and to keep this evidence
tuple fixed while realising the item. A deterministic verifier
recomputes the returned tuple against the bundle and admits the item
only if every component reproduces, with no repair and no human
judgement. In a paired experiment over 24 figure-bearing chunks from
scanned Vietnamese-law holdings, the prompt is the only quantity that
varies: the contracted prompt admits $22/24$ items against $16/24$ and
$12/24$ for two control prompts, with both paired contrasts significant
under multiplicity control, and it eliminates quotation from text that
is rendered only inside page images.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction and Related Work}
Scanned textbooks and institutional documents combine prose, diagrams,
and tables, motivating textbook QA~\cite{kembhavi2017tqa} and its
generation counterpart. A fluent generated question may still assert
unsupported content~\cite{ji2023hallucination} or may only appear
multimodal while remaining answerable from text
alone~\cite{goyal2017making,agrawal2018don}. Existing document models
fuse text, layout, and pixels~\cite{xu2020layoutlm,xu2021layoutlmv2,
huang2022layoutlmv3} or read pixels directly~\cite{kim2022donut,
lee2023pix2struct}, while DocVQA and ChartQA assess
answers~\cite{mathew2021docvqa,masry2022chartqa}; educational and
visual question generation motivate the construction
task~\cite{heilman2010good,kurdi2020systematic,mostafazadeh2016generating}.

Our contribution is the ECM--TQAG protocol itself: evidence support is
stated as a contract on one generation call, and that contract is made
checkable by a deterministic verifier that recomputes the declared
evidence against the source. The protocol is evaluated by a paired
design in which each source chunk serves as its own control, so any
difference in the measured outcome is attributed to the contract alone.
Section~\ref{sec:method} specifies the protocol,
Section~\ref{sec:setup} instantiates the experiment, and
Sections~\ref{sec:results}--\ref{sec:discussion} report the census.

\section{Method}
\label{sec:method}
ECM--TQAG has three components: a conditioning bundle that fixes what
may serve as evidence, an evidence contract that constrains how an item
is produced from that bundle, and a deterministic verifier that turns
each returned item into a binary outcome. Fig.~\ref{fig:method} shows
the architecture, including the data pipeline that builds the bundles.

\subsection{Conditioning bundle}
Each source chunk $c$ is represented by
\begin{equation}
E_c=\bigl(T_c,\;L_c,\;\mathcal I_c\bigr),
\end{equation}
where $T_c$ is the recognised page text, $L_c$ the document structure
(headings, figure and table regions), and $\mathcal I_c$ the set of
figure crops bound by content hash. Evidence outside $E_c$ is
inadmissible for a chunk, so the contract is checkable rather than
declarative.

\subsection{Evidence contract}
One multimodal generator $g$ is fixed for the whole protocol. Under arm
$a\in\mathcal A=\{\textsc{ecm},\textsc{dir},\textsc{str}\}$, one call
produces one candidate item,
\begin{equation}
y_{c,a}=g\bigl(\pi_a,\;E_c\bigr),
\label{eq:call}
\end{equation}
where $\pi_a$ is the arm prompt and the decoding settings, the
question type $t_c$, and the response schema are identical across arms.
The ECM contract adds four operations to that shared instruction: it
selects a verbatim quotation $q$ from $T_c$ together with a figure $h$
from $\mathcal I_c$; it plans the answer $\alpha$ before wording the
question; it holds the evidence tuple
\begin{equation}
\tau=(t,\;q,\;\alpha,\;h)
\end{equation}
fixed while the item is realised; and it self-checks schema
conformance. All four operations are instructions within the single
call of \eqref{eq:call}; because the provider returns only the final
structured item, intermediate reasoning never enters the evidence
record. The two control arms
share the same call structure; the direct arm simply requests one
multimodal item, and the structured no-contract arm additionally
emphasises exact schema completion.

\subsection{Deterministic verifier}
Verification recomputes $\tau(y)$ against $E_c$. The gate is the
conjunction
\begin{equation}
\begin{split}
G(y,E_c)=\;&\mathbb 1\{t=t_c\}\cdot
\mathbb 1\{q\sqsubseteq T_c\}\cdot
\mathbb 1\{h\in\mathcal I_c\}\\
&\cdot\,\mathbb 1\{\sigma(y)\}\cdot
\mathbb 1\{\mu(y)\},
\end{split}
\label{eq:gate}
\end{equation}
where $q\sqsubseteq T_c$ requires the quotation to be a contiguous
substring of the recognised text, $\sigma$ is exact schema conformance
with nonempty question, answer, visual description, and rationale, and
$\mu$ requires, for multiple choice, four distinct options whose marked
option equals $\alpha$. Because $G$ is evaluated on the response as
returned, admission is a property of the response itself. The mechanical admission of
chunk $c$ under arm $a$ is
\begin{equation}
A(c,a)=G\bigl(y_{c,a},E_c\bigr),
\label{eq:admission}
\end{equation}
computed entirely by the verifier. Passing $G$ certifies source
consistency only; it does not establish truth, legal correctness, or
pedagogical value.

\subsection{Paired identification}
Because $g$, $E_c$, $t_c$, the schema, and the decoding settings are
common to all arms, the paired difference $A(c,\textsc{ecm})-A(c,a')$
varies only with the prompt. Each chunk therefore acts as its own
control, and the contract is tested by exact paired comparisons with
prespecified multiplicity control. In addition, two arm-blind model
raters from independent families supply secondary semantic scores;
these are treated as exploratory in this work.

\section{Experimental Setup}
\label{sec:setup}
\paragraph{Material.}
The frame is a bounded census of 24 figure-bearing chunks, not a
population sample. The chunks come from scanned holdings of the
University of Law, Hue University: reference monographs, conference
proceedings, course textbooks, student research reports, undergraduate
theses, and institutional process and form documents, used for internal
research only; no page image is redistributed. The frame retains chunks whose figure is present, whose crop is
resolvable, and whose text is long enough to support a verbatim
quotation (Fig.~\ref{fig:method}).

\paragraph{Arms and models.}
Each chunk receives one call under each of the three arms of
\eqref{eq:call}. The generator is Qwen3-VL-8B-Instruct, held fixed
across arms so that no model change can confound the prompt contrast.
The two arm-blind raters are Claude Opus 5 and GPT-5.6 Sol, from
independent model families.

\paragraph{Quantities and tests.}
The measured outcome is the mechanical admission $A$ of
\eqref{eq:admission}, reported over all assigned chunks: a chunk whose
item fails any gate condition contributes zero to the rate. Each paired contrast compares the contracted arm with one control arm
by an exact McNemar test; the two $p$-values are adjusted by Holm's
step-down procedure at family-wise $\alpha=0.05$ (thresholds $0.025$
and $0.05$).

\section{Conclusion}
ECM--TQAG states evidence support as a contract on one generation call
and makes that contract checkable by recomputing the declared evidence
tuple against the source bundle. In a paired census of 24
figure-bearing chunks in which the prompt is the only quantity that
varies, the contract significantly raises the rate at which a single
call yields a source-consistent item, and it eliminates the dominant
failure mode of the control arms, namely quotation from text rendered
only inside page images. Admission certifies source consistency rather
than truth or pedagogical value, and extending the protocol to semantic
validity at scale remains open.

\section*{Data Availability}
The evidence consists of the frozen protocol, the evaluation instrument,
